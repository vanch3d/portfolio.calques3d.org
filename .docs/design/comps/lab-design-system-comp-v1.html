Design System

The Construction on Tracing Paper

A geometric proof in progress — compass arcs, dimension lines, and construction marks on cream draughting paper. Every element is placed with the deliberateness of a technical drawing: no decoration that isn't also structural, no colour that isn't also information.

Named Rules

The One Red Rule

The compass-arc red appears exactly once per surface — on the single element that is active, current, or selected.

A second red on the same surface means the first was wrong. Its rarity is the point: it marks the foreground construction element, nothing else.

The No-Decoration Rule

If removing an element makes the surface less informative, it was earning its place. If it makes it calmer, it was decoration.

Every mark on the drawing earns its place or is removed. This is not minimalism — it is maximum information, zero noise.

The Flat-by-Construction Rule

No shadows. Depth from line weight and tonal ramp only — heavy lines are closer, ghost lines are background.

A shadow here would be a smudge on the drawing. Depth is achieved through graphite weight hierarchy: heavy (1.5px), medium (1px), ghost (0.5px).

Colors

One accent used by rule. All other colour is tonal variation of graphite on cream. No colour enters the system without a named rationale for what information it carries.

Neutral

Draughting Paper

--color-ground #f8f4ed

The ground. Every surface begins here. Warm, not clinical — the colour of cream draughting paper, not office-white. The word calques (French: tracing paper) is the name of the PhD software; this ground makes that etymology visible.

Construction Graphite

--color-ink #2a2a2a

Primary lines, headings, body text. The weight of a freshly-sharpened pencil. Not pure black — graphite on cream reads as precision, not harshness.

Faded Graphite

--color-ink-secondary #6b6b6b

Secondary annotations, labels, dates. The weight of a second-pass construction line — used for Departure Mono labels, supporting metadata, and dimension annotations.

Ghost Line

--color-ink-ghost #c8c4bc

Tertiary grid lines, dividers, construction guides. The weight of a preliminary mark — present enough to structure the page, quiet enough not to compete with content.

Accent

Compass-Arc Red

--color-active #c0392b

The single active construction element per surface — current position, selected era, focused project. Never used for decoration. Its rarity is the point: when the eye finds it, it has found the foreground element.
