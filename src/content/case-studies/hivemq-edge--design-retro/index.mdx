# HiveMQ Edge: A Design Retrospective

Over three years at HiveMQ, I made the same design bet twice: that a graph-canvas paradigm was the right way to configure an industrial IoT gateway. I got two different outcomes. This is the record of both, what held up, what didn't, why, and what I actually do differently now, including how I work with AI agents on real production problems, not just how I'd describe it in an interview.

Six sections, roughly twenty-five minutes end to end. Short on time? Read the Problem Class, then Workspace and DataHub together, they carry the core argument. Everything after that goes deeper: into a harder representation problem underneath both features, into how I actually build day to day, and into what the practice looks like now.
