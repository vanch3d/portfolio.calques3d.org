# HiveMQ Edge: A Design Retrospective

Over three years at HiveMQ, I made the same design bet twice: that a graph-canvas paradigm was the right way to configure an industrial IoT gateway. The two bets had different outcomes, and this is the record of both: what held up, what did not, why, and what I do differently now, including how I work with AI agents on real production problems.

Six sections, roughly twenty-five minutes end to end. For a shorter route, read the Problem Class, then Workspace and DataHub together, since they carry the core argument. Everything after that goes deeper: into a harder representation problem underneath both features, into how I build day to day, and into what the practice looks like now.
