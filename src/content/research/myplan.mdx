The JISC-funded MyPlan project, based at the London Knowledge Lab (Birkbeck College) under
Prof Alex Poulovassilis, aimed to support independent lifelong learners by allowing them to
record, share, and discover learning pathways — structured records of educational and personal
episodes that shaped their development.

My responsibility in the second phase of the project was to implement personalisation
functionalities for the web-based application: the ability to search for and receive
recommendations of learning pathways through Further and Higher Education courses, matched to the
learner's own pathway. This involved adapting techniques from user profiling and string-metric
similarity matching to compare the semi-structured learning history records of different users.

A significant part of the work involved redesigning the web interface to support timeline-based
visualisation of learning pathways, enabling learners to manipulate and compare their trajectory
with others'. The system was evaluated through usability and formative evaluation sessions, and
a final evaluation report was produced as a JISC deliverable.
