Making Stuff emerged from two overlapping contexts: the "Making Things" workshop culture at
the London Knowledge Lab (LKL), and the Making Stuff collective at the Learning Sciences
Research Institute (LSRI), Nottingham — a group of researchers and PhD students building
creative pieces out of interactive digital technologies.

At LKL, collaboration with Dr Brock Craft and Dr Philip Kent produced the _Spot-On_ project,
a BECTA Research Grant-funded exploration of wireless sensor motes (Sun SPOT API) and the
combination of physical and digital representations for discovering spherical triangles
(WiiMote IR camera and IR pens with Processing). This collaboration was presented at the
ShareIt Workshop on Shareable Interfaces for Learning (2008).

At LSRI Nottingham, the Making Stuff collective took an exploratory and informal approach —
brainstorming and designing prototypes to various degrees of finalisation:

- **Magic Cushion** — Phidgets and RFID tags, Processing/PowerPoint visualisation, supporting story-telling
- **Pong** — Phidgets and pressure sensors, a physical user interface for the Pong game, promoting engagement in atypical scenarios
- **Chalk n' Talk** — Phidgets and touch sensor, physical activation of audio streams hidden on a blackboard
- **Weatherman** — Processing visualisation of environmental data as a web mashup
- **Falling Apple** — WiiMote IR camera and cap-mounted IR emitters, floor projection, interactive physical-digital game (specified but not implemented)

The work was driven by curiosity about embodied and kinaesthetic learning rather than formal
research hypotheses — a deliberate counterpoint to the more structured clinical work on
auditory games running in parallel at Nottingham.
