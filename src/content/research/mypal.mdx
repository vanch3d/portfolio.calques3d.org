The myPAL project was the principal research and development output of my University Academic
Fellowship in Mobile and Technology Enhanced Learning at the Leeds Institute of Medical Education
(LIME), University of Leeds.

The context was a Faculty of Medicine with over 3,000 undergraduate students using mobile devices
as part of their medical education across multiple cohorts (MBChB). Technology Enhanced Learning
had grown significantly, but the feedback mechanisms available to students and staff remained
generic and unsystematic. The position aimed at delivering state-of-the-art, data-driven, personalised
feedback.

My approach was to design a Learning Analytics-based personalised feedback system that could operate
at scale across live student cohorts. This required both a data infrastructure and a web-based
interface layer. I advocated for the adoption of Learning Locker (LRS) and the xAPI specification
as the backbone for managing learning and performance data — allowing fine-grained event tracking
across diverse learning activities.

The proof-of-concept ecosystem was built with a microservices architecture, RESTful API, LAMP
stack, and MongoDB, with a frontend using Slim PHP, ES6, d3.js, Bootstrap v3, and CSS3/SCSS. The
framework for applying data analytics to learning experience in vocational contexts was published
at the IMS workshop at ITS'16 (2016).
